Your grading scale

–
German grade

Enter your CGPA to see the conversion.

German universities convert foreign grades with the modified Bavarian formula: 1.0 is the best grade and 4.0 the lowest pass. Your marks memo states your scale; most Indian universities use 10 and a pass grade of 4, 5 or 5.5.
