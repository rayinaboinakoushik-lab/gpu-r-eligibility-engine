Privacy notice

GPU-R admission check, test version. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Koushik Rayinaboina, Berlin, Germany. GPU-R is a personal research project, not a company and not a university service. Contact: rayinaboinakoushik@gmail.com

What happens to your data

Your marks memo (and syllabus pages, if you add them) Read once to find your courses, credits, grades and degree details. The file is deleted right after it has been read.
What is read from it Your course list, grade values and the results of the check. Kept on the server until you click Delete my data, and deleted automatically 30 days after you started the check at the latest.
Anonymous run statistics One row per check: a random run ID, the date, whether the check finished, the subject field, how many courses and results it had, how long it took and which version of GPU-R ran. No name, grades, course titles or files. These rows are kept, to show how GPU-R is used and how well it works.
Course knowledge Course titles and the subject areas they belong to (and, if you add syllabus pages, a short quote describing a course) may be kept so the same course is recognised faster for later students. This is never linked to you.
In your browser Only the ID of your current check, stored in this browser tab until you close it. No cookies, no tracking, no advertising.

Who else processes it

  • OpenAI (USA) reads your marks memo for GPU-R through its API. According to OpenAI's API terms at the time of writing, data sent through the API is not used to train its models and may be kept for up to 30 days to detect abuse. This means your memo is processed outside the EU.
  • Render hosts the website on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, and keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as the hosting provider.

Why it is allowed

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given with the checkbox before upload. You can withdraw it at any time: click Delete my data on the results page, or write to the contact address above.

Your rights

You can ask what is stored about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to a data protection authority, in Berlin the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

Please note

GPU-R gives guidance, not an admission decision. Each university decides on its own; check the programme page before you apply.
